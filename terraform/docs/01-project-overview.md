# NovaSupply Platform

## Project Overview

NovaSupply is a cloud-native Supply Chain Management and Procurement Platform designed for global enterprises.

The platform enables organizations to manage:

- Supplier Management
- Procurement Operations
- Inventory Visibility
- Warehouse Management
- Logistics Tracking
- Demand Forecasting
- Cost Optimization
- Analytics and Reporting

---

## Business Objective

Provide a scalable, secure, and highly available platform for managing end-to-end supply chain operations across multiple regions.
